PRIVACY POLICY — yalla-nel3b

Last Updated: 6th December, 2025

Welcome to yalla-nel3b (“we,” “our,” “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our mobile application yalla-nel3b, where players can join voice rooms, play the Jackarro game, chat with friends, and manage their profiles.

By using our app, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1.3 Gameplay Information

  • In-app actions and progress

1.4 Device & Technical Information

  • Device model & operating system

  • App version
  • Crash logs & performance analytics

Collected for security, optimization, and user experience improvement.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the Jackarro game and voice-room features
  • Enable chat, friends, and social interactions
  • Personalize the player experience
  • Improve gameplay quality and performance
  • Detect fraud or misuse

  • Provide customer support using Info@yalanel3b.com

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share limited data with:

  • Service providers (analytics, hosting, moderation tools)

All third-party partners comply with necessary privacy standards.
